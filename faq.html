Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with me

I specialize in full-stack web development using the Next.js + Supabase + TypeScript ecosystem. My core stack includes Next.js (App Router, server components, API routes), TypeScript, Supabase (PostgreSQL, real-time subscriptions, authentication), and modern CSS (Tailwind CSS, CSS custom properties). I also have experience with React, Node.js, and various frontend technologies. For backend logic, I work with PostgreSQL, REST APIs, and serverless functions.

My process typically follows five phases: (1) Discovery — understanding your requirements, goals, and target audience; (2) Planning — creating a project roadmap, technology decisions, and timeline; (3) Design — wireframing and UI/UX design with your feedback; (4) Development — building the application with regular progress updates; (5) Deployment & Handoff — launching the project and providing documentation. Throughout the process, I maintain clear communication and welcome your input at every stage.

Timelines vary depending on the scope and complexity of the project. A simple landing page or brochure website can take 3-5 days. A medium-complexity web application with database integration and user authentication may take 2-3 weeks. Larger full-stack applications with multiple features, admin dashboards, and third-party integrations can take 4-8 weeks. After our initial consultation, I provide a detailed timeline estimate tailored to your specific project requirements.

Pricing depends on the project scope, complexity, and timeline. I offer both fixed-price and hourly arrangements. Fixed-price projects work well for well-defined scopes, while hourly arrangements suit ongoing work or projects with evolving requirements. I provide a detailed quote after understanding your project needs. My rates are competitive while reflecting the quality and expertise I bring to each project. Contact me for a personalized quote.

I provide a support period after project delivery to ensure everything runs smoothly. This includes bug fixes, minor adjustments, and deployment support. For ongoing maintenance, I offer monthly retainer packages that cover updates, security patches, performance optimization, and feature additions. I'm also available for one-off maintenance tasks as needed. My goal is to build a long-term relationship and be there whenever you need technical assistance.

I'm currently accepting new projects and clients. My typical response time is within 24 hours. I work flexible hours and can accommodate different time zones. For urgent inquiries, WhatsApp or phone calls get the fastest response. I believe in maintaining a manageable workload to ensure each project receives the attention it deserves. Reach out and I'll let you know my current availability for starting new work.
